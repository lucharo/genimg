# Answered design record

Use one record per load-bearing decision. Keep it short enough to scan cold.

```markdown
# ADR-NNNN: <decision>

- Status: Accepted
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Outcome

<What the user will see or be able to do.>

## Answered grill

- **What problem are we solving?** <answer>
- **Why this shape?** <answer>
- **What owns the data?** <answer>
- **What happens at the boundary?** <answer>
- **What are we deliberately not building?** <answer>
- **What would make us revisit this?** <answer>

## Decision

<The accepted contract in precise terms.>

## Consequences

- <positive consequence>
- <trade-off or operational obligation>

## Rejected alternatives

- **<alternative>:** <why it loses for this contract>
```

Prefer the user's canonical vocabulary. Do not turn settled product direction back into an open questionnaire.
